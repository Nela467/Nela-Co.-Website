About Us

Hi Everyone! I created Nela & Co - Rise to Balance because I believe wellness is not about perfection; it is about connection, growth, and balance in every part of life.

This page is my little corner of the internet where health, mindfulness, and movement meet in a way that is real, practical, and uplifting.

Here you will find:
- Delicious easy-to-follow recipes that nourish your body and soul.
- Workout videos for every level so you can move confidently and joyfully.
- Mindfulness and wellness practices to calm your mind and centre your spirit.
- A supportive community where we lift each other up, share wins, and grow together.

My goal is simple: To inspire you to rise each day feeling balanced, strong, and empowered — body, mind, and heart. No extremes, no quick fixes, just sustainable habits and a space to celebrate progress, not perfection. Join me, explore, and let's rise to balance together!
